# Supermarket Sales Analysis Using Python

## Project Overview
This project analyzes supermarket sales and profit data using Python. It explores monthly sales trends, product category performance, profit patterns, and monthly sales growth through data visualization.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze monthly total sales.
- Compare sales across product categories.
- Understand monthly profit trends.
- Compare sales and profit.
- Calculate monthly sales growth percentages.
- Identify useful business insights from the data.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib

## Dataset Description
The dataset contains six months of sample supermarket data, from January to June.

Columns include:
- Month
- Electronics
- Clothing
- Furniture
- Profit
- Total Sales
- Sales Growth Percentage

## Key Findings
- Total sales across the six months were ₹4,02,000.
- Electronics generated the highest category sales at ₹2,03,000.
- Furniture generated ₹83,000 in sales.
- June recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹88,000.
- June recorded the highest monthly profit at ₹17,000.
- February had the highest positive monthly sales growth at 20%.
- March sales decreased by approximately 3.33% compared with February.

## Visualizations
The project includes five visualizations:
1. Monthly Total Sales Analysis
2. Category-wise Sales Analysis
3. Monthly Profit Analysis
4. Monthly Sales vs Profit Comparison
5. Monthly Sales Growth Analysis

## Business Recommendations
- Monitor electronics inventory to meet customer demand.
- Investigate reasons for the March sales decline.
- Monitor sales and profit trends regularly.
- Evaluate product category performance before making inventory decisions.
- Analyze product costs and profit margins before deciding which categories to prioritize.
